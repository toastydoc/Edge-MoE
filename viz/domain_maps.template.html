Where the router sends each domain

The router sits at the centre. Each ring going outward is one of the 30 layers, holding its 128 experts. Bolts trace the experts a domain is routed to, layer by layer. Every ring has 128 evenly spaced slots. By default an expert's slot depends on which domains use it more than the others do, so each domain's experts lean toward its own side of the circle; switch to expert-number order to see the raw layout.
